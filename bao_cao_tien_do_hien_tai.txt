1. TOM TAT DIEU HANH

Du an da xay dung duoc nen tang ky thuat quan trong cho chuoi xu ly du bao:
thu nhan da nguon, chuan hoa va truy vet du lieu, feature engineering, dataset
chuoi, baseline/LSTM/Transformer, dong goi model, API va cac thanh phan van
hanh co ban. Kiem thu lich su ghi nhan 49 test dat. Tuy nhien, lan kiem chay
gan nhat chua hoan thanh do cau hinh moi truong DEBUG=release khong hop le voi
truong Boolean debug.

Danh gia tong quat: Du an dang o giai doan prototype nghien cuu va hoan thien
ky thuat. Chua du bang chung de cong bo la san pham van hanh, do chua co bao
cao hieu nang tren du lieu thuc, uncertainty chua duoc calibration va dashboard
/kenh canh bao chua hoan thien.

2. CAC HANG MUC DA HOAN THANH

2.1. Nen tang du an va moi truong

- Da khai bao Python 3.11 va pyproject.toml lam nguon dependency chinh.
- Da phan tach dependency cho API, training, ingestion, experiment va dev.
- Da co Makefile, Dockerfile, Docker Compose, .env.example, CONTRIBUTING va
  cau truc src/ theo module.
- Da khai bao PostGIS, Redis va MinIO cho moi truong phat trien.
- Da co cac quy uoc format/lint/test voi Black, isort, flake8, pytest va
  pre-commit.

2.2. Thu nhan va quan ly du lieu

- Da co parser/adapter cho CMA, JTWC, JMA, IBTrACS va NCHMF.
- Da co parser nhan tac dong PCTT, luu tach khoi quan sat track.
- Da chuan hoa storm ID va yeu cau timestamp co timezone UTC.
- Da luu provenance: source, URL, SHA-256 tep nguon, thoi diem tai ve va
  metadata lien quan.
- Da ghi Parquet phan vung theo source/nam UTC va co co che ghi atomic.
- Da co co che merge da nguon: doi chieu storm ID, dung sai 3 gio va 75 km;
  uu tien IBTrACS > JTWC > JMA > CMA > NCHMF; giu lai conflict metadata.

2.3. Lam sach va feature engineering

- Da xay dung canonical bridge tu raw observation sang bang track sach.
- Da chuan hoa don vi gio va ap suat, mapping intensity code theo nguon.
- Da co cac feature vi tri, lag, dong hoc, thoi gian, mua bao, khoang cach
  den bo bien Viet Nam/diem tham chieu va dac trung cuong do.
- Da dung mot FeatureBuilder chung cho train va inference.
- Da co FeatureScaler fit tren train-only, kiem tra schema cot va xu ly NaN,
  Inf, cot suy bien.
- Da xu ly truong hop timestamp trung de tranh tao toc do/gia toc gia.

2.4. Dataset, mo hinh va huan luyen

- Da co TyphoonDataset voi cua so dau vao va multi-horizon cau hinh duoc.
- Da co split theo toan bo storm ID hoac nam, tranh ro ri giua train/test.
- Da co persistence/motion baseline, LSTM, Seq2Seq/Attention LSTM,
  Transformer va CLIPER baseline trong codebase.
- Da co loss ket hop hoi quy vi tri va phan loai cuong do.
- Da co trainer, early stopping, gradient clipping, scheduler, checkpoint va
  resume checkpoint.
- Da co metric Haversine track error, along/cross-track error va intensity
  accuracy theo horizon.
- Da co config mau LSTM: input 4 buoc, horizon 6/12/24/48/72 gio, 2 layer,
  hidden size 128, dropout 0.2, toi da 100 epoch.

2.5. Dong goi model, inference va API

- Da co model bundle gom weights, scaler, split manifest va manifest co hash.
- Da kiem tra checksum, feature schema, model constructor va horizon khi nap.
- Da co inference bang trained bundle hoac motion baseline co canh bao.
- Da co FastAPI endpoint health, ready, version, forecast, active typhoons,
  track, impact minh hoa, observations, subscriptions, alerts va metrics.
- Da co validation dau vao nghiem ngat cho toa do, timestamp, horizon, toc do
  di chuyen va thu tu quan sat.
- Da co API key, so sanh hmac, rate limit co ban, security headers va cache.

2.6. Van hanh

- Da co operational store va worker cap nhat du lieu/forecast.
- Da co co che subscription va alert draft idempotent.
- Worker duoc thiet ke de khong gui thong bao ra dich vu ben ngoai.
- Da co cron configuration cho data update scheduler.

- RUI RO CAU HINH: DEBUG=release dang chan test. Muc do: cao trong ngan han.
  Giam thieu: tach ro APP_ENV va DEBUG; chi cho phep true/false cho DEBUG.
- RUI RO DU LIEU: Du lieu da nguon co the mau thuan hoac thieu. Muc do: cao.
  Giam thieu: bao cao chat luong, review conflict va theo doi coverage.
- RUI RO MO HINH: Chua co metric tren tap du lieu thuc holdout. Muc do: cao.
  Giam thieu: thiet lap baseline, split theo bao/nam, bao cao metric cong khai.
- RUI RO BAT DINH: Cone hien chua calibrated. Muc do: cao neu hien thi cho
  nguoi dung. Giam thieu: ghi ro "illustrative/not calibrated" va calibration.
- RUI RO VAN HANH: Chua co quy trinh release/monitoring/rollback day du.
  Muc do: trung binh-cao. Giam thieu: CI, registry approval, observability.
- RUI RO TRUYEN THONG: Nguoi dung co the hieu nham la canh bao chinh thuc.
  Muc do: cao. Giam thieu: disclaimer bat buoc va tham chieu NCHMF.
