BAO CAO CHI TIET DU AN
HE THONG HO TRO DU BAO DUONG DI BAO VIET NAM

1. THONG TIN CHUNG

Ten du an: Typhoon VN Forecast System
Phien ban khai bao: 0.1.0
Ngon ngu va nen tang: Python 3.11, PyTorch, FastAPI, Pydantic, Pandas, NumPy.
Giay phep: MIT.
Thoi diem danh gia: 19/09/2026.

Muc tieu cua du an la xay dung he thong ho tro ra quyet dinh cho viec du bao
duong di xoay thuan nhiet doi co kha nang anh huong Viet Nam va Bien Dong.
He thong xu ly du lieu best-track va du lieu moi truong, tao dac trung, huan
luyen mo hinh du bao nhieu moc thoi gian, cung cap API va co so de hien thi
ban do/canh bao.

Gioi han an toan: San pham chi mang tinh chat tham khao va ho tro nghiep vu.
No khong phai ban tin du bao khi tuong chinh thuc. Moi ket qua tra ra phai
kem nguon du lieu, thoi diem phat hanh, phien ban mo hinh, thong tin bat dinh
va canh bao nguoi dung tuan theo huong dan cua NCHMF.

2. BAI TOAN VA PHAM VI

Du an giai quyet bai toan du bao vi tri tam bao trong cac moc 6, 12, 24, 48
va 72 gio. Dau vao la chuoi quan sat gan nhat cua mot con bao: thoi gian UTC,
vi do, kinh do, gio, ap suat, cap cuong do va nguon du lieu. Dau ra gom cac
diem du bao, thoi diem hieu luc, nhan cuong do, ban kinh bat dinh, vung cone
dang GeoJSON, phien ban mo hinh, phien ban du lieu va canh bao han che.

Pham vi da duoc thiet ke:
- Thu nhan, phan tich va bao toan nguon goc du lieu bao.
- Lam sach, hop nhat da nguon, tao dac trung khong gian - thoi gian.
- Xay dung dataset chuoi, chia du lieu khong lam ro ri thong tin giua bao.
- Huan luyen baseline va mo hinh hoc sau; danh gia bang sai so khoang cach.
- Dong goi mo hinh co checksum, phuc vu du bao qua HTTP API.
- Luu tru quan sat, cache ket qua, canh bao nhap va ha tang phat trien Docker.

Ngoai pham vi hien tai:
- Phat hanh canh bao khi tuong chinh thuc.
- Gui SMS, email, Zalo, Telegram hoac push notification thuc te. Ma nguon chi
  tao ban nhap canh bao, khong tu dong gui ra ben ngoai.
- Danh gia tac dong day du ve gio, mua, lu, song, do bo hoac cap tinh. Endpoint
  impact chi tinh khoang cach diem den duong du bao minh hoa.
- Chung nhan van hanh, calibration thong ke day du va tich hop dashboard web
  hoan chinh trong phien danh gia nay.

3. KIEN TRUC TONG THE

Luong xu ly logic:

CMA / JTWC / JMA / IBTrACS / NCHMF / ERA5 / NOAA OISST
  -> ingestion va kiem tra
  -> chuan hoa, khop trung va luu tru co phien ban
  -> feature builder va feature store
  -> train, danh gia, model bundle/registry
  -> FastAPI inference
  -> dashboard, danh gia diem gan va canh bao nhap.

Cac thanh phan duoc tach thanh module doc lap, vi vay co the kiem thu, thay
the va trien khai rieng:
- ingestion: tai, parse, chuan hoa va luu nguon du lieu.
- features: lam sach, chuyen doi du lieu quan sat va tao bien dau vao.
- datasets: tao cua so chuoi thoi gian va tach tap train/validation/test.
- models va training: baseline, LSTM, Transformer, loss, checkpoint, bundle.
- evaluation: sai so track, sai so doc/ngang track va do chinh xac cuong do.
- inference va api: nap artifact, kiem tra dau vao, du bao va public HTTP API.
- operations: luu tru van hanh, xu ly cap nhat va tao alert draft.
- infra: Dockerfile, Docker Compose va cron scheduler.

4. DU LIEU VA QUAN TRI NGUON GOC

Nguon du lieu duoc tich hop/thiet ke adapter gom CMA, JTWC, JMA, IBTrACS va
NCHMF; nhan tac dong PCTT duoc luu tach khoi track. Roadmap con du kien dung
ERA5, NOAA OISST va du lieu du bao moi truong. Du lieu thuan theo WGS84 va
thoi gian UTC.

Moi Observation bat buoc co source, source_storm_id, storm_id, timestamp,
latitude, longitude, source_url va SHA-256 cua tep nguon. He thong tu choi:
- toa do nam ngoai gioi han WGS84;
- timestamp khong co timezone;
- gia tri wind/pressure khong huu han;
- don vi gio khong nam trong tap da ho tro (kt, knot, knots, m/s, ms).

Raw observation duoc ghi thanh Parquet, phan vung theo nguon va nam UTC. Qua
trinh ghi dung tep tam roi doi ten, giup tranh sinh part file do dang khi ghi
loi. File nguon duoc bam SHA-256 de co the truy vet chinh xac object da parse.

Khi hop nhat da nguon, hai quan sat chi duoc xem la trung neu cung storm_id da
duoc resolve, chenh thoi gian khong qua 3 gio va cach nhau khong qua 75 km.
Thu tu uu tien la IBTrACS, JTWC, JMA, CMA, NCHMF. Gia tri mau thuan khong bi
trung binh hoa; ban ghi duoc chon giu thong tin cac nguon khac trong metadata
source_conflicts. Cach lam nay uu tien kha nang kiem toan hon la che giau sai
khac giua cac co quan.

5. LAM SACH VA TAO DAC TRUNG

FeatureBuilder cung cap mot duong tao dac trung dung chung cho huan luyen va
suy luan, giam nguy co training-serving skew. Danh sach feature co thu tu co
dinh va gom:
- Vi tri hien tai, chenh lech vi do/kinh do va do tre 1-3 buoc.
- Dong hoc: quang duong buoc, bearing, toc do km/h, gia toc, goc re va toc do
  re. Timestamp trung/lui khong bi gan khoang cach 6 gio gia tao.
- Thoi gian: sin/cos cua gio, ngay, thang, ngay trong nam; co in_season.
- Dia ly: khoang cach, bearing toi bo bien Viet Nam va cac diem tham chieu.
- Cuong do: wind m/s, pressure hPa, bien doi gio/ap suat va one-hot cap bao.

FeatureScaler chi fit tren tap train, luu thu tu cot, gia tri bu, mean va scale
de dung lai o validation, test va inference. Scaler tu choi schema thieu, thua
hoac doi thu tu cot; xu ly cot toan NaN/Inf de khong lam nhiem ket qua.

6. DATASET, MO HINH VA HUAN LUYEN

Dataset tao cua so dau vao co cau hinh duoc. Cau hinh LSTM mau dung 4 quan sat
cach nhau 6 gio va du bao cac horizon 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 buoc, tuong ung 6-72 gio.
Du lieu duoc tach theo toan bo storm_id hoac theo nam, tranh dua cac diem cua
cung mot con bao vao ca train va test.

Mo hinh va baseline hien co:
- Persistence/motion baseline dung huong va toc do cua hai quan sat gan nhat.
- LSTMTrackForecaster: du bao dong thoi toa do va lop cuong do nhieu horizon.
- Seq2Seq LSTM va Attention LSTM trong khung mo hinh.
- TransformerTrackForecaster cho thu nghiem chuoi thoi gian.
- CLIPERForecaster lam baseline khi so sanh mo hinh.

Loss ket hop hoi quy vi tri va phan loai cuong do; ho tro Smooth L1 hoac
Haversine cho phan hoi quy. Cau hinh mau su dung LSTM 2 layer, hidden size
128, dropout 0.2, Adam learning rate 0.001, weight decay 1e-5, gradient clip
1.0, scheduler cosine, toi da 100 epoch va early stopping patience 10.

Metric danh gia gom track error bang khoang cach Haversine (km), thanh phan
sai so doc track/ngang track va accuracy cuong do theo tung horizon.

7. MODEL BUNDLE VA TINH TAI LAP

Model sau huan luyen duoc dong goi thanh thu muc gom weights.pt, scaler.json,
split-manifest.json va manifest.json. Manifest luu phien ban, dataset version,
SHA-256 dataset, cau hinh model, input length, horizon, feature schema hash,
nhan cuong do, ban kinh validation va checksum tung file.

Khi nap model, he thong allowlist loai LSTM, kiem tra day du schema manifest,
checksum, kich thuoc feature, horizon, so lop dau ra va scaler state. PyTorch
duoc nap voi weights_only=True. Do do artifact bi thay the hoac sai contract
se bi tu choi truoc khi phuc vu du bao.

Ban kinh uncertainty cua trained model hien duoc mo ta la percentile sai so
radial tren validation, chua phai do bao phu da calibration. Baseline dung ban
kinh minh hoa max(30 km, 5 km x so gio). Ca hai deu gan canh bao ro rang.

8. API VA VAN HANH

FastAPI cung cap cac endpoint:
- GET /health: trang thai tien trinh va moi truong.
- GET /ready: kiem tra ket noi database va version forecaster.
- GET /version: version ung dung, model, loai model va dataset.
- POST /forecast: du bao theo quan sat gui len hoac track da luu.
- GET /typhoons/active va /typhoons/{storm_id}/track.
- GET /typhoons/{storm_id}/impact: khoang cach diem den cac forecast point.
- POST /observations: ghi batch quan sat va cap nhat du bao.
- POST /subscriptions, GET /alerts: quan ly subscription va alert draft.
- GET /metrics: metric dem request/error/cache, co bao ve xac thuc.

API validate nghiem ngat timestamp UTC, vi do/kinh do, gio, ap suat, cuong do,
storm ID, thu tu quan sat va horizon hop le. Mot request du bao phai co it nhat
2 quan sat; interval gan nhat phai trong 0-24 gio; toc do suy ra phai <=150
km/h; timestamp qua tuong lai bi tu choi. Ket qua cu khong qua 24 gio duoc gan
canh bao freshness.

Bao ve van hanh hien co API key X-API-Key, compare_digest, rate limit theo IP,
gioi han cardinality, security headers nosniff/same-origin/DENY va cache database.
Redis la tuy chon; khi Redis loi, database cache van duoc dung. Production bat
buoc co API_KEY va khong duoc cho phep baseline.

Worker validate toan bo snapshot truoc khi ghi, upsert quan sat, tao forecast,
so sanh cone voi ban kinh subscription va luu alert draft idempotent. Worker
khong gui thong bao ra he thong ben ngoai.

9. HA TANG VA PHAT TRIEN

Du an dung pyproject.toml lam khai bao dependency chinh. Nhom api bao gom
FastAPI/Uvicorn/SQLAlchemy/PostgreSQL/Redis; train gom PyTorch, Pandas,
scikit-learn; ingestion gom cdsapi, netCDF4, pyarrow, xarray; experiment dung
DVC va MLflow; dev co pytest, black, isort, flake8 va pre-commit.

Docker Compose dinh nghia API, PostGIS 16, Redis 7.4 va MinIO. Day la ha tang
phat trien, voi credential mac dinh local-only; khong duoc dua credential nay
vao moi truong chia se/production. Scheduler cron co file cau hinh rieng.

11. DANH GIA HIEN TRANG

Da co nen tang ky thuat tot cho proof-of-concept va nghien cuu: contract du
lieu ro rang, truy vet SHA-256, xu ly ro ri train/test, feature dung chung,
bundle co kiem tra toan ven, API validation va guardrail an toan. Ma nguon API
va inference hien da ton tai, do do mo ta cu trong README rang API chi co
health/version da khong con phan anh day du codebase hien tai.

Chua du dieu kien de coi la he thong canh bao van hanh: chua co chung nhan
accuracy tren du lieu thuc te cong bo, uncertainty chua calibration, dashboard
chua duoc kiem chung hoan chinh, delivery canh bao chua duoc phep/gui thuc,
Docker Desktop chua duoc chay tai workspace theo README, va full suite dang
bi chan boi cau hinh DEBUG.

12. RUI RO VA KHUYEN NGHI

- Khac biet nguon bao: Giua CMA/JTWC/JMA/IBTrACS co the lech thoi diem/vi tri.
  Can luu conflict, review mau va lap bao cao chat luong theo nguon.
- Ro ri thoi gian: Phai duy tri split theo storm hoac nam va fit scaler train-only.
- Generalization: Synthetic test khong thay the validation bang bao lich su thuc.
  Can cong bo error km theo 6/12/24/48/72h va theo khu vuc/mua.
- Uncertainty: Cone hien chua la xac suat bao phu da hieu chinh. Can calibration,
  coverage check, reliability report va nguong chan release.
- Operational safety: Khong duoc goi endpoint impact la du bao tac dong. Can
  giu disclaimer, provenance, model version va thong tin freshness tren UI.
- Cau hinh: Tach app_env (vi du development/release) va debug (true/false),
  bo bien DEBUG=release hoac sua script moi truong truoc CI/CD.
- Bao mat: Xoay API key, thay credential Docker mac dinh, dat secret trong kho
  secret cua moi truong trien khai va bo sung rate-limit phan tan neu scale ngang.
